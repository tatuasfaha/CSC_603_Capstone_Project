# S03 Allergy

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/allergy.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Allergies involve an immune response to substances such as pollen, dust mites, mold, or pet dander. Symptoms vary. Identifying and limiting exposure to a known trigger can help. A severe allergic reaction requires emergency care; routine allergy advice should never replace urgent help.
