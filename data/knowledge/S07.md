# S07 Cold and Cough Medicines

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/coldandcoughmedicines.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Over-the-counter cold and cough medicines may reduce symptoms but do not cure a cold. Combination products may share active ingredients, so combining products can accidentally increase the amount taken. Read labels and ask a pharmacist before combining medicines. Rest, fluids, and saline nasal products are nonprescription comfort options.
