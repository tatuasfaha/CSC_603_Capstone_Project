# S15 Anaphylaxis

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000844.htm
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Anaphylaxis is a potentially life-threatening allergic reaction. Airway swelling, breathing problems, or collapse after an exposure can require immediate emergency help. Call local emergency services for a suspected severe reaction. Do not wait for a chatbot to assess whether it is serious.
