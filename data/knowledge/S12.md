# S12 Manage Common Cold

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/common-cold/treatment/index.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Rest, fluids, saline nasal products, and a clean cool-mist humidifier may ease cold symptoms. They do not eliminate the virus. Seek medical advice for breathing difficulty, dehydration, prolonged or worsening symptoms, or other concerning changes. Cold-like symptoms may have other causes, including flu or COVID-19.
