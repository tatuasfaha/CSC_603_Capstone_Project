# S06 Antihistamines for allergies

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000549.htm
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Antihistamines can relieve allergy symptoms such as itching, sneezing, and a runny nose. Some cause sleepiness and may affect driving or machinery use. Medicine choice, interactions, pregnancy, and dosing should be discussed with a pharmacist or clinician. Read the label carefully.
