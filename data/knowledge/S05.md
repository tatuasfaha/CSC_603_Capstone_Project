# S05 Allergic rhinitis self-care

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000547.htm
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Reducing exposure to known allergens is part of allergy self-care. Antihistamines and some nasal sprays may relieve symptoms, but suitability depends on the person and product. Some medicines cause drowsiness. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about choices and follow the product label rather than using chatbot dosing.
