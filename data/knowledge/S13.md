# S13 Preventing Spread of Respiratory Viruses When You Are Sick

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/respiratory-viruses/prevention/precautions-when-sick.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

For respiratory illness, CDC advises staying home and away from others until symptoms are improving overall and fever has been absent for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medicine. Added precautions for the next five days can reduce spread. Healthcare settings have separate guidance; consult current official guidance for details.
