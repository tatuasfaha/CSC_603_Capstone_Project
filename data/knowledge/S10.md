# S10 People at Increased Risk for Flu Complications

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/flu/highrisk/index.htm
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Some groups have greater risk of serious flu complications, including adults 65 and older, pregnant people, and people with certain chronic conditions or weakened immunity. They should contact a healthcare professional promptly for suspected flu rather than relying on a chatbot for treatment decisions.
