# S11 Treatment of Flu

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/flu/treatment/index.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Prescription antiviral medicines may be used for flu. They tend to offer the most benefit when started early, and a clinician should decide whether they are appropriate. People with severe illness or increased risk should seek prompt advice. Antibiotics do not treat influenza viruses.
