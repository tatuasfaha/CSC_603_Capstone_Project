# S02 Flu

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/flu.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Influenza is a respiratory infection caused by influenza viruses. It can be mild or serious. Fever, body aches, fatigue, cough, and sore throat can occur, but symptoms alone cannot reliably distinguish flu from other respiratory illnesses. A healthcare professional can advise about testing and treatment.
