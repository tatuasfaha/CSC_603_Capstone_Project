# S08 Signs and Symptoms of Flu

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/flu/signs-symptoms/index.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Respiratory illness warning signs in adults include breathing difficulty, persistent chest or abdominal discomfort, confusion, seizures, lack of urination, or marked weakness. Symptoms that improve and then worsen can also need immediate care. Seek medical help promptly for severe or concerning symptoms. A short checklist is not exhaustive.
