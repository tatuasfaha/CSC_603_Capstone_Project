# S01 Common Cold

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/commoncold.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Colds are viral infections affecting the nose and throat. Symptoms can include a runny or blocked nose, sneezing, cough, and sore throat. Different infections can cause similar symptoms, so a description alone does not confirm a cold. Rest and fluids can help with comfort; consult a professional about concerning symptoms.
