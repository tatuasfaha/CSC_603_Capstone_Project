# S04 Allergic rhinitis

Source: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000813.htm
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Allergic rhinitis can cause sneezing, an itchy nose or eyes, watery eyes, and nasal congestion after allergen exposure. Pollen is a common seasonal trigger. A clinician can help determine whether symptoms are allergic and discuss appropriate treatment. Symptoms are not a definitive diagnosis.
