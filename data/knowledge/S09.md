# S09 Flu What To Do If You Get Sick

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/flu/takingcare/
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Many mild flu illnesses can be managed at home, but severe illness and higher-risk situations need professional advice. Avoid close contact with others while sick. Contact a clinician early if at higher risk or worried about symptoms. Emergency warning signs warrant emergency medical attention.
