# S14 Antibiotic Do's and Don'ts

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/about/index.html
Source checked: 2026-11-15
Type: Short AI-drafted paraphrase. Team review pending.

Antibiotics treat certain bacterial infections and do not treat viruses that cause colds or flu. Taking them unnecessarily can cause harm. Do not share antibiotics or use leftover prescriptions. A healthcare professional should determine whether an antibiotic is needed.
